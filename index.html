The idea is simple

Nobody quits because an adult told them to.

Dear Fellow Scroller is a newsletter written by teenagers who are tired of losing hours to apps built to keep them there. Each letter is one person, writing honestly to someone just like them. The people who write the letters end up scrolling less themselves, because it’s hard to tell someone else to put the phone down and then pick yours back up.

A few things to know

Fair questions.

Is this anti–social media?

No. It’s anti-autopilot. The goal is to cut back on, or delete, the apps that eat your time, not to disappear from the internet.

Who writes the letters?

Teenagers who want to scroll less, writing to other teenagers who want the same.

Do I need to quit to join?

No. You just need to want to scroll less.

What happens to my name and email?

Only the founder sees them. They’re never published, sold, or connected to anything you write unless you explicitly say so. No cookies, no analytics, no trackers on this site. Ask and your info is deleted.
